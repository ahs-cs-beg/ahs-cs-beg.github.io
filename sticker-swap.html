Sticker Swap: Matching Two Lists

You and Jay both collect stickers. Use keep to find the stickers you both have, and the ones only one of you has.

Your stickers are in a list called mine. Jay's are in theirs. There are six levels. Do them in order. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Level 1: Stickers we BOTH have

Keep asks a question about each sticker in mine, one at a time. Here the question is: "Does Jay's list contain this sticker?"

Predict first. Click the stickers in mine you think keep will report. Then step through it.

Level 2: Build the BOTH block

Put the pieces in the right spots. Click Run to try it on the stickers. It has to work on a second pair of albums too.

Goal: report the stickers that are in BOTH albums.

Level 3: Stickers only I have

Trade time. Jay wants the stickers you have that Jay does NOT have. Same keep, same question, but wrapped in not, so the answer flips.

Predict first. Click the stickers in mine you think keep will report.

Level 4: Build it for any goal

Now you choose whether to use not. Get three goals right to finish the level.

Level 5: What does it report?

Read the block. Click the stickers it reports, then check. Get four right to finish the level.

Level 6: Not just stickers

Same two blocks, different lists. Pick the block that answers the question. Get four right to finish.
