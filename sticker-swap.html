Sticker Swap: Matching Two Lists

You and Jay both collect stickers. Loop through one list to find the stickers you both have, and the ones only one of you has.

Your stickers are in a list called mine. Jay's are in theirs. You will loop through one list, ask if the other list contains each sticker, and add the ones you want to a new list m. There are eight levels. Do them in order. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Level 1: for each, stickers we BOTH have

for each hands you the stickers in mine one at a time, in the variable item. The if asks: does Jay's list contain this sticker? If yes, it gets added to m.

Predict first. Click the stickers you think end up in m. Then step through it.

Level 2: Build it with for each

Put the pieces in the right spots and click Run. It has to work on a second pair of albums too.

Goal: report the stickers that are in BOTH albums.

Level 3: for i, the same job by counting

for i does not hand you the sticker. It hands you a number: 1, then 2, then 3, up to the length of the list. You get the sticker yourself with item (i) of (mine).

Predict first. Click the stickers you think end up in m.

Level 4: Build it with for i

Careful: i is a number, not a sticker.

Goal: report the stickers that are in BOTH albums.

Level 5: Stickers only I have

Trade time. Jay wants the stickers you have that Jay does NOT have. Same loop, same question, but wrapped in not, so the answer flips.

Predict first. Click the stickers you think end up in m.

Level 6: Build it for any goal

Now you choose whether to use not, with both kinds of loop. Get three goals right to finish the level.

Level 7: What ends up in m?

Read the script. Click the stickers it reports, then check. Get four right to finish the level.

Level 8: Not just stickers

Same loops, different lists. Pick the script that answers the question. Get four right to finish.
